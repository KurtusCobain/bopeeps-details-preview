Privacy Information

A straightforward overview of how this website works and the third-party services used for booking, directions, and social media.

Information handled by this website

Like most websites, basic request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and technical connection details may be processed by the website hosting and network providers as needed to deliver the site securely.

BoPeeps does not use this static website to store payment-card information. Appointment scheduling and any Booksy account or checkout activity take place through Booksy's services.

Third-party services and links

The site links to or embeds services operated by third parties, including Booksy for appointment booking, Google Maps or Google directions for navigation, and Facebook for BoPeeps social media.

Those services have their own privacy practices and terms. BoPeeps does not control how those third parties process information after you open or interact with their services.

Cookies, advertising, and analytics

BoPeeps does not currently use this website for mailing-list signups, first-party analytics, or a separate advertising platform. Third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when their content or booking tools are loaded.

Questions

For questions about this website or this privacy notice, contact BoPeeps at hello@bopeepsdetails.com or call 980-598-1864.

For questions about Booksy account, booking, or payment data, use Booksy's own support and privacy resources.
