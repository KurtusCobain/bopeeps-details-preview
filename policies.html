Policies & Service Information

This page is the home for pricing, vehicle condition, booking, payment, and other service policies so important details stay easy to find without crowding the main website.

Current policy

Pricing & Vehicle Condition

Service prices reflect standard vehicle conditions. Excessive pet hair requiring additional removal time may incur a $20 pet hair removal fee. If applied, the charge will be itemized in your final checkout and reflected on your receipt or payment confirmation email.

When does the fee apply?

The fee is intended for excessive pet hair that requires additional removal time beyond normal detailing. A few stray hairs are not the intended threshold.

How is it shown at checkout?

If the additional service is required, a separate $20 Excessive Pet Hair Removal charge is added to the final appointment amount and reflected on the receipt or payment confirmation email.

Additional service, booking, payment, cancellation, or vehicle-condition policies can be added to this page as BoPeeps expands its policies.
